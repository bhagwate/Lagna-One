About the Author

Mr. Partho Banerjee is a Jyotiṣa teacher, researcher and author with a long-standing professional background in software and technology.

His engagement with Jyotiṣa developed alongside his professional career rather than as a replacement for it, eventually becoming a sustained field of research, guidance and teaching.

He is an Indian astrologer practicing Nadi jyotiṣa and Parashara integrated astrology system. His passion in jyotiṣa is dedicated making actionable jyotiṣa software tools accessible to all.

This Astrology software is created, owned, managed and maintained by Partho Banerjee, that’s me. LagnaOne is designed to grow with and for the Jyotiṣa community.

Astrology: My journey in Jyotiṣa or Indian astrology, numerology and cosmic sciences began in the year 1999 and I started informal horoscope analysis in the year 2001. All through that decade, I was pursuing my full time job in the IT along-side my quest in astrology in parallel.

My journey in IT: I have been a full time IT and software professional for 14 years and have done Master's in Computer Applications. I am certified corporate IT trainer by Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur, India and IBM Corporation. I have worked with leading IT companies in banking and financial services domain at global cities including London, Singapore, Mumbai, Copenhagen, Hong Kong etc.

In my eventful journey of 24 years in astrology, I have done consultations for people from different walks of life across various cultures and countries (thanks to the internet).

In my view, Astrology is all about understanding root cause analysis and karmic healing required to handle/help in major obstacles in life - child birth issues, delayed marriage or serious relationship/marriage issues, career counseling and opportunities/challenge analysis, legal disputes, debts and loans, or other long/short term planning and decisions in life.
